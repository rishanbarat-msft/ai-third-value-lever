01 · The AI Landscape

South Africa: Wired Fast, But Capability-Constrained

The infrastructure is arriving quickly. The binding constraint for your portfolio companies is people and data… not access.

Infrastructure is landing

Africa's largest data-centre market (~$2.2bn). Cloud regions from Microsoft, Google, AWS, Oracle. Cassava–Nvidia "AI Factory" brings sovereign GPU capacity onshore.

Policy is catching up

Draft National AI Policy in Cabinet process; sector-specific, multi-regulator model anchored to POPIA & King V. Finalisation targeted 2026/27.

Sovereignty is a theme

Push to keep data and "intelligence" within borders; models tuned to local languages (Zulu, Afrikaans). Foreign-led infrastructure raises a dependence question.

Skills are the bottleneck

60%+ of SA/African firms cite the skills gap as a top barrier to transformation; only ~5% of African AI practitioners have access to real compute.

So the SA opportunity for private capital isn't to bet on the infrastructure race — it's to apply AI inside the businesses you already own.

03 · Risks for Private Capital

The Deal: Disruption Cuts Both Ways

The same force that creates value can quietly erode it. Underwrite for both.

Moat disruption & mispricing

AI will transform some businesses and undercut others' models. That makes pricing harder and adds a question to every diligence:

"Could this company's moat be disrupted by AI within the hold period?"

If you can't answer it, you're carrying unpriced risk.

Execution risk: the 95% trap

Most pilots never reach the P&L. The risk isn't doing nothing — it's funding motion that looks like progress. And the agentic shift raises the stakes: agents that act, not just assist, need governance before scale.

Demand proof of P&L impact, not demos.

Governance, Talent & the Social Contract

Three risks that are easy to underweight — and costly to ignore in this market.

Governance & POPIA

AI regulation is coming via existing regulators. Data governance, explainability and algorithmic decisions move from optional to expected.

Vendor & sovereignty

Foreign-led infrastructure and model dependence create lock-in and concentration risk. Ask where the data — and the value — ultimately sits.

Talent — the real ceiling

35% cite talent as the #1 barrier to scaling AI. In SA the skills gap is sharper. Capability, not capital, is the binding constraint.

The social contract. For capital that answers to working families, AI cannot be only a cost-and-headcount story. The durable framing is augmentation and growth — win more work, serve more customers, protect and create jobs.

An Ultra, Not a Sprint

AI value creation is won in the unglamorous base work and the discipline to keep executing after the early adrenaline fades. It punishes anyone who goes out too hard on the hype.

The winners pace, instrument, and compound.
